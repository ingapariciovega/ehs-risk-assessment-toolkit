# Action review record

Use explicit states: **Proposed → Assigned → Implemented → Effectiveness checked → Closed**. Reopen an action if conditions change or the control is ineffective.

For each transition record the owner, date and supporting evidence. Installation or training attendance proves an activity occurred; it does not by itself prove exposure was controlled. Keep evidence of what changed and how that was checked. Operational authorization remains in the site's established process.

Before closure: Were exposed workers involved? Was the control actually implemented? Is effectiveness supported by observations or measurements appropriate to the hazard? Are there new hazards or maintenance needs? Who accepted the closure under the local procedure?
